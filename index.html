Radar + SquadUp

Live on Render
24-Hour Hackathon · KR Mangalam University · October 2026 · Team of 2

Events struggle to discover the right audience, while attendees frequently suffer from low engagement and high drop-off rates. Radar + SquadUp tackles both ends of the funnel in one automated flow.

1. Radar (Targeted Outreach) Scans and identifies online communities where your audience already gathers, then drafts tailored outreach messages ready for organizer approval.

2. SquadUp (Community Engagement) Automatically sorts registered attendees into team channels, generates team identity cards, sends invites, and re-engages inactive participants.
